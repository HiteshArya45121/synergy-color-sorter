Smart Color Selection Mode

Shape Selection Mode

Injection System

Using high-quality solenoid valve, the frequency can reach 1200-1500Hz, while ensuring the output, the output ratio is smaller. Japan cooperated in the development of depassing technology which has lower power consumption and longer life.

Operating System

The newly optimized operation interface is simple and easy to learn. The system automatically detects when it is turned on. Equipped with a panoramic intelligent analysis system. Discharging System

Adopting the design of feeding protection and soft landing buffer, according to the different needs of domestic and foreign customers, the discharge hopper has two options: "large hopper" and each group of independent "small hopper". Image Acquisition System

The international cutting-edge industry professional full-color high-speed line scan imaging CCD sensor, and the color sorting special lens tailored by the top international supplier can accurately identify the subtle bacteria of 0.03mm2, and meet the high-end color sorting requirements of heterogeneous and heterogeneous simultaneous sorting and handling.

Light Source

Intelligent LED light source is steplessly adjusted, which can match the various brightness requirements of different materials. It is designed with cold light source system to have a longer life, high luminous efficiency and strong stability. It can be equipped with RGB variable color background lights for better selection.
